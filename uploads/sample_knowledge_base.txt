BizCorp Ethiopia — Customer Support Knowledge Base
===================================================

COMPANY OVERVIEW
----------------
BizCorp Ethiopia is a leading technology distribution company based in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. We supply electronics, office equipment, and business solutions to enterprises across the country. Our mission is to empower Ethiopian businesses with cutting-edge technology at competitive prices.

Contact: support@bizcorp-et.com | Phone: +251-11-555-0100 | Hours: Mon–Fri 8AM–6PM EAT


RETURN & REFUND POLICY
-----------------------
- Items can be returned within 30 days of purchase with original receipt.
- Products must be in original, unused condition with all accessories and packaging.
- Electronics (laptops, phones, printers) have a 14-day return window due to manufacturer requirements.
- Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days via the original payment method.
- Defective items are eligible for exchange or full refund within 90 days of purchase.
- Custom orders and software licenses are non-refundable.
- To initiate a return, contact returns@bizcorp-et.com with your order number.


WARRANTY INFORMATION
--------------------
- All products carry the manufacturer's original warranty.
- Laptops & Computers: 1-year manufacturer warranty + optional 2-year BizCorp extended warranty.
- Mobile Phones: 1-year manufacturer warranty.
- Printers: 1-year warranty, with optional 3-year service contract.
- Accessories (mice, keyboards, cables): 6-month warranty.
- Warranty claims require proof of purchase and original packaging.
- Warranty covers manufacturing defects only; physical damage, water damage, and misuse are excluded.
- For warranty service, visit our service center at Bole Road, Addis Ababa, or call +251-11-555-0200.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
---------------------------

Q: Can I track my order?
A: Yes. Once your order ships, you receive an SMS with a tracking number. Visit track.bizcorp-et.com or call +251-11-555-0100 with your order number.

Q: Do you offer bulk/corporate discounts?
A: Yes. Orders above 50,000 ETB receive 5% discount. Orders above 200,000 ETB receive 10% discount. Government and NGO institutions receive 8% standard discount with a formal purchase order.

Q: What is the warranty claim process?
A: (1) Call our warranty hotline or visit any branch. (2) Bring the product and original receipt. (3) Our technician assesses the defect (free of charge). (4) If covered, repair or replacement is completed within 5–10 business days.

Q: Do you have a loyalty program?
A: Yes — BizRewards. Every 1,000 ETB spent earns 10 points. 1,000 points = 100 ETB voucher. Sign up at bizcorp-et.com/rewards or at any branch.

Q: Can I reserve/pre-order products?
A: Yes. A 30% deposit is required for pre-orders. Products are reserved for 14 days. If not collected, the reservation is cancelled and the deposit refunded within 3 business days.

Q: What if my product arrives damaged?
A: Immediately photograph the damage and contact us within 48 hours of delivery. We will arrange free pickup and send a replacement or issue a full refund. Claims after 48 hours may not be accepted.
